# Ham Bacon Cheese Sandwich

## Overview
- 2 pieces of bread
- 2 pieces of ham 
- 2 pieces of bacon

## Assumptions 
- The robot is standing in front of a kitchen counter.
- All items are on the counter: 
- One loaf of sliced bread inside a sealed plastic bag.
- A package of cooked bacon sealed in a plastic container. 
- A package of sliced ham sealed in a plastic container. 
- One plate on the counter.
- The robot's are empty. 
- The robot can grab, pull, open containers, and place items when instructed literally. 
- The bacon and ham are already fully cooked.
- the robot does not understand any action unless it is explicitly described. 

# Instructions
1. Place your hand on the plastic bread bag.
2. Grip the bread bag firmly.
3. Locate the twist tie or plastic clip with your left hand.
4. Remove the twist tie or clip by unwrapping or unclipping it. 
5. Pull the bread bag open by seperating the plastic edges.
6. Insert your right hand into the bread bag and grasp one slice of bread. 
7. Remove the slice and place it on the plate.
8. Insert your right hand into the bag again and grasp a second slice of bread. 
9. Remove the slice and place it on the plate next to the first slice. 
10. Place your right hand on the bacon container lid. 
11. Lift the lid upward until itn seperates from the container. 
12. Set the lid on the counter.
13. Use your right hand to pick up one strip of cooked bacon.
14. Place the bacon strip on top of the first slice of bread.
15. Pick up a second strip of bacon. 
16. Place the second strip of bacon. 
17. Place your right hand on the ham container lid. 
18. Lift the lid upward until seperates from the container. 
19. Set the lid on the counter. 
20. Use your right hand to pick up one slice of ham
21. Place the ham slice on top of the bacon
22. Pick up a second slice of ham. 
23. Place the second ham slice on top of the firsst ham slice
24. Pick up the second slice of bread using your right hand. 
25. Place it face-down on top of the ham so the plain side faces outward
26. press gently to join the slices
27. Place the completed sandwich back on the plate 

## Done when 
- A completed ham and bacon sandwich is assembled and resting on the plate.
